# Room illustration photos

`room-pool.webp`, `room-office.webp` and `room-hobby.webp` are cropped, resized and colour-graded versions of photos
from Pexels (https://www.pexels.com/license/ — free to use and modify): photo ids 261102, 667838 and 4050318.
They are generic illustrations, never the owner's home. The other `room-*.webp` come from the AUREON design package.

2026-11-06: sharper versions. The design-package rooms are now encoded from their 1672×941 originals (WebP q90, no
downscaling); pool, office and hobby are re-cut at 2560×1440 from the same Pexels photos and matched to the earlier
colour grade.

2026-11-10: the nine design-package rooms (bathroom, living, bedroom, kitchen, dining, balcony, hallway, entrance,
control room) are upscaled 2× from their 1672×941 originals with Real-ESRGAN x2plus (BSD-3-Clause model, run offline),
then resized to 2560×1440 (WebP q86). Same pictures and style as before, only sharper.
